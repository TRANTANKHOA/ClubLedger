1️⃣

Declare Expense

Admin taps "Declare Cost Item" in Budgets, selects category (Pitch, Tournament, Equipment, Referee), enters amount, and notes description.

2️⃣

Attach Proof

Admin links the official receipt proof (e.g. Pitch_Rental_Signed_Invoice.pdf or receipt screenshot URL).

3️⃣

Inspect & Verify

Members tap "View Attached Proof" in their invoices to inspect the receipt, review calculation math, or initiate a dispute ticket.
