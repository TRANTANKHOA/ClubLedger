✨ Overview & System Mission
Module 1

ClubLedger is a sports team management and transparent accounting application for recreational clubs, competitive squads, and leagues. It completely eliminates financial friction and post-season disputes through real-time attendance verification, automated cost allocation, and receipt sharing.

⚖️

Proportional Cost Engine

Splits pitch rents and league fees by exact attendance ratios: (Member Sessions ÷ Total Sessions) × Incurred Cost. Zero attendance equals $0 charge.

🛡️

Immutable Ledger & Audits

Every transaction, invoice allocation, payment approval, and dispute resolution creates timestamped, tamper-proof debit and credit ledger rows.

📱

Dual Role Architecture

Single app binary with instant seamless toggle between Squad Member Mode and Team / Global Admin Mode with superset governance.

🧾

Proof & Receipt Sharing

Admins declare expenses and attach signed PDFs/receipt screenshots. All members have access to transparently inspect receipts in-app.

🔗

Secure Invite Links

One-tap shareable join links and numeric codes with admin approval toggles for rapid squad onboarding and roster synchronization.

⚡

1-Tap Quick-Marking

Captains and Coaches can mark an entire squad session in seconds, with bulk approval, single-player check-in, and dispute adjudication.

💡
Core Design Principle: Zero Assumption & Full Transparency

No club member is ever charged a flat estimate. Incurred costs are backed by uploaded receipts, and dues are mathematically locked to coach-verified session attendance records.
