🛡️ Distributed Defense & Security Governance
Module 4

ClubLedger implements a defense-in-depth model safeguarding financial funds, attendance records, and administrative authority against fraud, accidental double-charging, and privilege escalation.

👑

Superset Role Hierarchy

Global Admin > Team Admin > Captain > Member. Admins inherit all member privileges plus audit dashboards and treasury management.

🔒

Two-Man Rule Verification

Payments logged by members require explicit Treasurer or Team Admin verification and approval before entering the valid treasury balance.

📝

Immutable Transaction Logs

Transactions are append-only. Balances cannot be manually rewritten without an explicit debit/credit entry accompanied by a mandatory audit reason.

⚖️

Built-in Dispute Mediation

Formal dispute workflows allow players to challenge attendance counts or billing amounts. Adjudications produce an automated ledger correction.

🔑

Granular Team Permissions

Team Admins can only view and manage rosters, budgets, and payments belonging to their designated team; cross-team leakage is strictly prevented.

🗄️

Database Migration Safety

Room schema migrations (v1 ➔ v2) utilize safe column alterations with backward compatibility and zero data loss on device upgrades.

🛡️
Security Assertion

No user, including team admins, can delete historical transactions. All accounting records remain verifiable for Annual General Meetings (AGM) and formal auditing.
