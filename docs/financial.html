💰 Financial Engine & Mathematical Definitions
Treasury Math

ClubLedger strictly adheres to formal mathematical definitions for sports club financial administration:

1. Total Monthly Budget (Sum of Incurred Costs)
Total Monthly Budget = ∑ (Incurred Cost Item_i) Total Monthly Budget = Pitch Rental + Referee Fees + Tournament Entry + Match Balls + Gear
The monthly budget is never an arbitrary estimate; it equals the sum of real expenses declared by team admins.
2. Total Member Collections
Total Member Collections = ∑ (Approved Member Deposits & Payment Dues)
The cumulative total of all verified funds contributed by squad players into the team treasury.
3. Net Team Treasury Position
Net Treasury Position = Total Member Collections − Total Incurred Costs • Surplus (> $0.00): Positive reserve carried forward • Deficit (< $0.00): Dues outstanding or pending collection
Displayed dynamically on the Team Treasury Position KPI card in the Admin Budgets screen.
4. Proportional Member Invoice Allocation
Member Charge = (Member Verified Attended Sessions ÷ Total Squad Sessions) × Incurred Cost • Zero Attendance Exemption: Attended = 0 ➔ Member Charge = $0.00
Ensures absolute fairness so players pay only for the games and training sessions they attend.
