✨ Overview & System Mission
Module 1

ClubLedger is a sports team management and transparent accounting application for recreational clubs, competitive squads, and leagues. It completely eliminates financial friction and post-season disputes through real-time attendance verification, automated cost allocation, and receipt sharing.

⚖️

Proportional Cost Engine

Splits pitch rents and league fees by exact attendance ratios: (Member Sessions ÷ Total Sessions) × Incurred Cost. Zero attendance equals $0 charge.

🛡️

Immutable Ledger & Audits

Every transaction, invoice allocation, payment approval, and dispute resolution creates timestamped, tamper-proof debit and credit ledger rows.

📱

Dual Role Architecture

Single app binary with instant seamless toggle between Squad Member Mode and Team / Global Admin Mode with superset governance.

🧾

Proof & Receipt Sharing

Admins declare expenses and attach signed PDFs/receipt screenshots. All members have access to transparently inspect receipts in-app.

🔗

Secure Invite Links

One-tap shareable join links and numeric codes with admin approval toggles for rapid squad onboarding and roster synchronization.

⚡

1-Tap Quick-Marking

Captains and Coaches can mark an entire squad session in seconds, with bulk approval, single-player check-in, and dispute adjudication.

💡
Core Design Principle: Zero Assumption & Full Transparency

No club member is ever charged a flat estimate. Incurred costs are backed by uploaded receipts, and dues are mathematically locked to coach-verified session attendance records.

🛡️ Distributed Defense & Security Governance
Module 4

ClubLedger implements a defense-in-depth model safeguarding financial funds, attendance records, and administrative authority against fraud, accidental double-charging, and privilege escalation.

👑

Superset Role Hierarchy

Global Admin > Team Admin > Captain > Member. Admins inherit all member privileges plus audit dashboards and treasury management.

🔒

Two-Man Rule Verification

Payments logged by members require explicit Treasurer or Team Admin verification and approval before entering the valid treasury balance.

📝

Immutable Transaction Logs

Transactions are append-only. Balances cannot be manually rewritten without an explicit debit/credit entry accompanied by a mandatory audit reason.

⚖️

Built-in Dispute Mediation

Formal dispute workflows allow players to challenge attendance counts or billing amounts. Adjudications produce an automated ledger correction.

🔑

Granular Team Permissions

Team Admins can only view and manage rosters, budgets, and payments belonging to their designated team; cross-team leakage is strictly prevented.

🗄️

Database Migration Safety

Room schema migrations (v1 ➔ v2) utilize safe column alterations with backward compatibility and zero data loss on device upgrades.

🛡️
Security Assertion

No user, including team admins, can delete historical transactions. All accounting records remain verifiable for Annual General Meetings (AGM) and formal auditing.

💰 Financial Engine & Mathematical Definitions
Treasury Math

ClubLedger strictly adheres to formal mathematical definitions for sports club financial administration:

1. Total Monthly Budget (Sum of Incurred Costs)
Total Monthly Budget = ∑ (Incurred Cost Item_i) Total Monthly Budget = Pitch Rental + Referee Fees + Tournament Entry + Match Balls + Gear
The monthly budget is never an arbitrary estimate; it equals the sum of real expenses declared by team admins.
2. Total Member Collections
Total Member Collections = ∑ (Approved Member Deposits & Payment Dues)
The cumulative total of all verified funds contributed by squad players into the team treasury.
3. Net Team Treasury Position
Net Treasury Position = Total Member Collections − Total Incurred Costs • Surplus (> $0.00): Positive reserve carried forward • Deficit (< $0.00): Dues outstanding or pending collection
Displayed dynamically on the Team Treasury Position KPI card in the Admin Budgets screen.
4. Proportional Member Invoice Allocation
Member Charge = (Member Verified Attended Sessions ÷ Total Squad Sessions) × Incurred Cost • Zero Attendance Exemption: Attended = 0 ➔ Member Charge = $0.00
Ensures absolute fairness so players pay only for the games and training sessions they attend.
